## icons_bencaos_lore

Use case: stylized-concept. Production raster game asset for Atriox dark fantasy action roguelite. Detailed hand-painted sharp outlined 2D game art, readable at small size, restrained luminous cores and crisp contours. REAL transparent alpha background, no black backdrop, no gradient backdrop, no checkerboard painted in. No text, labels, grid lines, scenery, character or weapon. Generous clear padding, never clip any effect. Create ONE UI icon atlas, exactly FOUR columns and TWO rows, equal square cells, seven highly polished distinct dark fantasy blessing emblems plus final bottom-right cell completely empty transparent. Transparent space between icons, no UI card, no frame, no badge background. Each icon same visual size, centered with padding. Read left to right top to bottom: 1 Disciplina de Althar: silver-blue three sword-strike notches connected by flowing dodge crescent, symbolic only. 2 Quebrador de Grilhões: broken bronze shackles with white shockwave. 3 Instinto do Eremita: moss-green hunter arrowhead nested in restrained ivory focusing arcs. 4 Cinzas de Ignacius: charred ember and small crimson-orange flame. 5 Cicatrizes Douradas: dark crimson fissure stitched together by radiant gold. 6 Vontade do Renegado: violet spectral crown split by a white upward slash. 7 Julgamento Próprio: ivory-blue parry star protected by two curved ward arcs. 8 empty. No letters or numbers. Use ornamental painterly fantasy shapes not vector pictograms.
